Temple Bizarre: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Temple Bizarre ("the game") is a puzzle game made by Jyoti Neupane ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: we do not collect your personal information. The only outside service is Google AdMob, which shows an ad only when you choose to watch one.

What the game stores

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, lives, settings, your explorer choice, the name you choose for yourself and your answer to the age question) is saved only on your own device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game, or "Delete my data" in Settings, deletes it.

Optional ads (Google AdMob)

Some buttons say "Watch ad" (for example for an extra life). An ad plays only when you tap one of these buttons; the game never shows ads by itself. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure an ad, Google may use information about your device such as its advertising identifier, IP address and general location, and how you interacted with the ad. How Google uses this is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

  • You choose: before any personalised ad, the iPhone asks whether you allow tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency), and players in the EU, UK and Switzerland are first shown Google's consent form. If you say no, ads are not personalised.
  • Children: the game asks for your age once, in a neutral way. Players under 13 are never shown the tracking question and only get non-personalised ads marked for children.
  • You can change tracking at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • No analytics tools besides what Google AdMob uses for the ads you choose to watch.
  • This version contains no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not in Apple's Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children; under-13s only receive non-personalised ads (see above).

Deleting your data

Use "Delete my data" in the game's Settings, or delete the game. To reset the advertising identifier, use iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you have any question about your data, contact us at the address below.
