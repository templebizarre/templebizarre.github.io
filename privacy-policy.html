Temple Bizarre: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Temple Bizarre ("the game") is a puzzle game made by Jyoti Neupane ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: the game does not collect, store or share your personal information.

What the game stores

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, lives, settings and your explorer choice) is saved only on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes this information.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • This version contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • This version contains no in-app purchases.

Future versions

A later update may add optional in-app purchases (handled by Apple; we never see your payment details) and optional rewarded ads. Before that update is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what those features use, and where the law requires it the game will ask for your permission first.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because all data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the game. If you have any question about your data, contact us at the address below.
