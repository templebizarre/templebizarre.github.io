Temple Bizarre: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at kriyamaanneupane@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the level number if your question is about a level.

Common questions

How do I get more lives? A life comes back every 20 minutes. Each level also gives you up to 3 free lives when you run out.

Can I switch between the male and female explorer? Yes: tap the gear (Settings) on the title screen.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress. You can also clear it in Settings with "Delete my data".

How do the daily reward and daily spin work? After level 3, a new gift is ready every day. Come back on the next day for a bigger gift; if you miss a day, the calendar starts again at Day 1. You also get one free spin of the wheel each day. The chance of every prize is shown under the wheel.

How do power-ups work? Match 4 in a line for a rolling boulder, a 2x2 square for a bat swarm, an L or T shape for a spike trap, and 5 in a line for the Eye of the Temple. Swap two power-ups together to merge them.
